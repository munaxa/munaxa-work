# Enterprise Architecture Specification
## Munaxa Work
### Phase 18 – Employee Self-Service (ESS)

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# IMPORTANT

This phase implements the Employee Self-Service application.

ESS is an application layer.

ESS owns no business logic.

ESS consumes Application Services exposed by business domains.

Business rules remain inside their owning domains.

---

# Prerequisites

Claude MUST complete every previous phase before beginning ESS.

---

# Objectives

Build the employee-facing application.

Provide employees with secure self-service access.

Reuse existing APIs and Application Services.

Provide a responsive experience across desktop, tablet and mobile.

---

# Non Goals

Do NOT duplicate

Business logic

Validation

Payroll calculations

Leave calculations

Attendance calculations

Approval logic

Workflow engine

Notification engine

ESS consumes existing functionality.

---

# Business Vision

Employees should be able to complete daily HR activities without HR intervention.

The portal provides access to information already owned by business domains.

# Architecture Decisions

## AD-001

ESS contains presentation logic only.

---

## AD-002

Every operation calls Application Services.

---

## AD-003

Permissions are enforced by Platform and backend APIs.

The UI never grants access by itself.

---

## AD-004

ESS never accesses repositories directly.

---

## AD-005

ESS never accesses databases.

---

## AD-006

All communication occurs through versioned REST APIs.

---

## AD-007

Offline support is limited to explicitly supported features.

Synchronization must resolve conflicts safely.
